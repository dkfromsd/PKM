# Optical Networking & Connectivity Ticker List (inputs_20260501_optics)

This list focuses on the "Connectivity Bottleneck" in AI data centers, specifically transceivers, switches, and precision manufacturing.

## 1. Networking & Switching
- **ANET** (Arista Networks) - Ethernet switching for Cloud/AI
- **CIEN** (Ciena) - Optical transport and routing
- **CRDO** (Credo Technology) - High-speed connectivity / DSPs

## 2. Optical Components & Materials
- **COHR** (Coherent) - Lasers, optics, and transceivers (800G/1.6T)
- **LITE** (Lumentum) - Photonics and optical components

## 3. High-Efficiency Manufacturing (Operating Leverage)
- **CLS** (Celestica) - AI server/networking manufacturing services
- **FN** (Fabrinet) - Precision optical and electronic manufacturing
- **APH** (Amphenol) - Interconnects, sensors, and antennas
