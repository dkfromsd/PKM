**Focus: "The High-Margin Plumbers"**

- **Operating Leverage (The "Lynch Favorite")**: He would be stunned by **CLS**. Revenue is growing at 50%+, but their costs are not. This is pure margin upside. "The company is making more money without having to build a dozen new factories."
- **Niche Dominance**: **FN** (Fabrinet) is the secret craftsman. The big tech firms trust them to build the most sensitive optical gear. It’s a "toll-bridge" for high-end photonics.

**Stance**: Bullish on **CLS** (incredible momentum/leverage) and **APH** (boring reliability). Cautious on **COHR/LITE** as their manufacturing is capital-intensive and margins can be lumpy.

### 2. Bill Ackman's Perspective (Moats & Best-in-Class Margins)

**Focus: "The Architectural Monopolies"**

- **Business Quality (Moat)**: Ackman would favor **ANET** and **APH**. Arista's software-driven networking (EOS) creates massive switching costs and an architectural moat against Cisco. Amphenol's thousands of patents and deep customer integration make them irreplaceable.
- **Predictable High Margins**: **ANET** is the gold standard here. Net margins are significantly higher than peers, and they run a lean operation. They don't have the overhead of traditional hardware firms.
- **Growth without Cost**: **CRDO** (Credo) would intrigue him because of its high margin profile (26% net) for a semiconductor player. It’s a capital-efficient design house scaling into a multi-billion dollar market.
- **Capital Allocation**: He would look at **CIEN**'s slower growth with skepticism unless they can prove a pivot to higher-margin data center interconnects (DCI) is working.

**Stance**: Highly Bullish on **ANET** (High Moat/High Margin). Bearish on the fragmented low-margin EMS players, though he might find **CLS** an exception due to its recent AI-led valuation re-rating.
